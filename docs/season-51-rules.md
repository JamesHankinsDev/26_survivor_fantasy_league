# Survivor Fantasy League — Season 51 Rules Update

## Why we're changing things

Season 50 effectively ended at Week 9 — the bottom 3 teams were 33+ points back of 5th place with no realistic path to recover. The goal for next season: **harder to fall out of contention, more strategic decisions each week, real comeback tools after the merge.**

A retroactive S50 simulation using the new scoring (vote-out -5, removed survived-episode, weekly score floor of 0) shows the 1st-to-8th spread shrinking from 77 → 55 pts and the 7th-vs-8th gap shrinking from 9 → 1 pt — without changing rank order. Combined with structural changes (more swaps, droppable voted-out castaways, captains, pick-em, merge advantages), bottom teams will have meaningfully better recovery paths.

---

## Scoring changes

| Event | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Voted out (penalty) | -10 | **-5** |
| Survived an episode | +1 | **removed** |
| Made final 3 | +5 | **+10** |
| Season winner | +10 | **+25** |
| Immunity win | +5 | unchanged |
| Team challenge win | +3 | unchanged |
| Found idol/advantage | +5 | unchanged |
| Used idol successfully | +3 | unchanged |
| Voted at tribal | +3 | unchanged |
| Fire-making win | +5 | unchanged |
| Made jury | +3 | unchanged |

**New: weekly score floor of 0.** No matter how brutal the week, your team can't go negative. Multiple vote-outs in a single bad week stop hurting once you hit zero.

---

## Roster changes

### Pre-merge
- **2 roster swaps per week** (was 1)
- **Voted-out castaways can be dropped** — frees the slot for a swap

### Post-merge
- **1 roster swap per week**
- **Voted-out castaways stay on your roster** as dead weight — you don't get to replace them. By finale you might be down to 1 or 2 active castaways carrying you home.

### No castaway exclusivity
Same as last season — multiple teams can roster the same person. No drafts, no waivers.

---

## NEW: The Captain

When you lock your roster each week, designate one castaway as **Captain**.
- Captain's points that week are **×2**
- If your Captain is voted out, the **-5 penalty is also doubled to -10** (real risk/reward)
- Pick a new Captain every week, no restriction on repeats

---

## NEW: Weekly Pick-em

Each week, alongside your roster lock, predict who'll be voted out:
- **+3 pts** for a correct pick
- **+2 contrarian bonus** if less than 25% of the league got it right
- Works regardless of your roster — even an eliminated team stays in play

---

## NEW: Merge Advantages

At the merge, **teams ranked 4th-8th each receive a secret advantage**. Top 3 get nothing.

| Rank | Powers received |
|---|---|
| **8th** | Hidden Immunity Idol (guaranteed) **+** 1 random other |
| 7th | 1 random |
| 6th | 1 random |
| 5th | 1 random |
| 4th | 1 random |
| 1st-3rd | nothing |

### The catalog (6 powers)

| Power | Effect | Wasted if... |
|---|---|---|
| **Hidden Immunity Idol** | Your next vote-out skips the -5 penalty; castaway auto-drops, freeing the slot | (always works) |
| **Steal a Swap** | Cancel a chosen team's swap this week | Target had no swap queued |
| **Captain Boost** | Your Captain scores ×3 instead of ×2 this week | Your Captain is voted out |
| **Sabotage Captain** | A chosen team's Captain reverts to ×1 (no bonus) this week | Their Captain is voted out OR they used Score Shield |
| **Score Shield** | Your weekly team-score is replaced by the league average team-score that week. Pick-em still scores normally on top | (always works, but you give up upside if your roster crushes it) |
| **Bonus Pick-em** | Submit two boot picks this week, keep the better one | (always works) |

### How they work

- The full catalog is public (in About). Who **has** what stays secret. Who **uses** what stays secret until the end-of-season recap.
- If you're hit, you get an in-app notification: *"an advantage was played against you this week."* No detail until the season-end reveal.
- One-week cooldown on hitting the same team twice
- Stacking allowed (two teams can target the same victim) but the 0-floor still applies
- **Must be used within 4 weeks of the merge or it expires**
- Notifications are in-app only — no push or email

### Score Shield — special notes
- Captain ×2 has no effect during a Score Shield week (your roster's events are bypassed entirely)
- Vote-out penalties are bypassed for the week (they're part of the rostered events being skipped) — **but** the eliminated castaway stays on your roster as dead weight (no auto-drop, unlike the Idol)
- If multiple teams use Shield in the same week, the average is computed from each team's "would-have-scored" totals first, then all Shield users get that average

## NEW: Find a Hidden Advantage

If your team is getting buried, you might find one yourself. Three trigger conditions — any one fires:

| Trigger | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Falling Behind** | End of week (Week 4+): you're in last place AND more than 10 points behind 7th |
| **Roster Massacre** | End of week: you lost a castaway to vote-out **this week AND the previous week** |
| **Cold Streak** | End of week: you've accumulated **3 zero-point weeks** since league scoring started |

### Shared rules
- Minimum **2-week cooldown** between any two finds (regardless of which trigger fires)
- **Total cap of 3 found advantages per season**
- **Cold Streak counter resets** after firing (need 3 *more* zeros to re-trigger)
- **Roster Massacre cooldown is 4 weeks** (otherwise rapid pre-merge eliminations could rapid-fire it)
- Pre-merge eligible — applies all season

### Worst-case math
8th place could finish the season holding **2 merge powers (Idol + random) + up to 3 found powers = 5 total deployable advantages**. Strong, but capped.
